# Chez Chiro — Research Brief (Phase 1)

Captured 19 Oct 2026. Sources: chezchiro.com (all 6 pages), Google Maps listing, Google Search public panel, TikTok, Facebook (public header only), Instagram (public grid), Google results.
Raw data: `research/data/menu.json` (full menu, structured) and `research/data/google-reviews.json` (all 54 Google reviews).

## 0. At a glance

- **What it is:** "Restau Chez Chiro", a Beninese home-cooking brand that started as a delivery-only "restaurant en ligne" for Cotonou and Abomey-Calavi. Registered 2021 (RCCM RB/ABC/21 B 4666), based in Godomey-Togoudo, run by Directrice Générale **Chirelle ASSONGBA**.
- **The surprise:** the brand is far bigger online than its website suggests: **TikTok 2.8M followers / 22.9M likes**, **Facebook 562,523 followers**, plus **Google 4.4★ (54 reviews)** and **Facebook 4.5/5 (16)**.
- **What's next for them:** "**La Manifestation — Du virtuel au réel**", Save the Date **4, 5, 6 Dec 2026**, on both their TikTok avatar and Facebook cover. The poster shows a physical "Restaurant Chez Chiro" storefront with a terrace. This most likely marks the move from online-only to a physical venue. **That is an inference to confirm with the owner.**
- **The gap:** a free Bootstrap template ("Restoran" by HTML Codex) with template hero imagery, a 10 MB homepage, no WhatsApp, no clickable phone, old 8-digit numbers, and generic testimonials. None of the brand's strength (TikTok audience, Barbecue d'Or 2022, upcoming event) shows on the site.
- **Pitch timing:** a new site live before 4 Dec 2026 is a natural deadline.

## 1. Identity & positioning

**Names in use**
- Logo and site: "Restaurant Chez Chiro".
- Facebook, TikTok and site copyright: "Restau Chez Chiro" / "RestauChezChiro".
- Google Maps: "Restaurant chez chiro".
- Spoken brand: "Chez Chiro".

**Who is behind it.** Chirelle ASSONGBA is listed as Directrice Générale in the mentions légales. "Chiro" looks like her nickname: a TikTok caption says "Chez Chiro **elle-même** sera présente dans son stand", and a reviewer writes "vous êtes là meilleur grand sœur". The brand is personality-led. *Confirm she is "Chiro" and is happy to be the face of the site.*

**How they describe themselves (verbatim)**
- Site hero: "Nous sommes un restaurant en ligne, nous livrons partout sur Cotonou et Calavi, livraison très rapide"
- TikTok bio: "Restau en Ligne. Livraison Rapide 🚚"
- Instagram bio: "Restaurant en ligne, livraison rapide et efficace"
- Facebook: "Restaurant en ligne, commandez vos repas au téléphone et faites vous livrer au bureau ou à la maison…"
- Facebook post headline: "MÊME LES YEUX FERMÉS, NOS PLATS TROUVENT LE MOYEN DE S'INVITER DANS VOS PENSÉES !"
- Event poster: "Du virtuel au réel"

**Cuisine.** Beninese and West African home cooking:
- Sauces: gbota (sheep's head), assrokouin, mouton, graine, arachide.
- Starches: télibo, akassa, piron, agbéli, wassa-wassa, attiéké, riz au gras, aloco.
- Braised proteins: poulet bicyclette, lapin, mouton, aileron, croupion de dinde, poisson silivi/tilapia.
- Street-food crossovers: chawarma, frites.
- A breakfast porridge (tapioriz au lait), local drinks (bissap, gingembre, tamarin, ananas) and dairy (yaourt, dèguè).

**Credentials**
- **Barbecue d'Or 2022: won**, per their own Facebook and TikTok posts ("En 2022, Chez Chiro remportait le Barbecue d'Or"). A Bénin Barbecue Facebook post shows them competing ("BARBECUE D'OR Vidéo 16 Chez Chiro"), and a TikTok is titled "Barbecue d'or 2021 highlights". The site claims "2 Prix Barbecue d'Or"; only the 2022 win is confirmed.
- "20k Clients satisfaits": site claim, unverified.
- "Cela fait près de 10 ans que vous et moi travaillons ensemble": pinned TikTok caption, 2 Oct 2026, 637K views. Unverified history claim.
- "FINAB Business Influence" trophy visual, Instagram, April 2024 ("Du 23 au 28 avril 2024, Place de l'Amazone"). Unclear whether nomination or award.
- Stand at **Cotonou Barbecue 2026**, 23 Jul – 2 Aug, Bidossessi. Also present at Bénin Barbecue (Instagram @l_vedette).

**Price position.** Affordable to mid-range:
- 2 000–4 000 FCFA per person (Google, "signalé par 3 personnes").
- Most mains have 3 000–6 000 FCFA tiers.
- Pack Banger: 12 000 FCFA.

**Tone of voice.** Warm, cheeky, emotional and faith-tinged ("Que Dieu bénisse mes abonnés, c'est grâce à vous 🙏🏽"). Emoji-heavy and community-first. Lots of direct address ("vous et moi"), teasers ("SOYEZ PRÊT 🔥🔥🔥"), humour ("Chez Chiro ferme ses portes 😂").

**Positioning proposal (for discussion, not a decision):** a founder-led Beninese kitchen with a 2.8M-strong TikTok community. Fast delivery across Cotonou & Calavi today, and from December a place to eat in. The site should turn TikTok attention into WhatsApp orders, and announce the venue and the event.

## 2. Likely clientele (hypotheses with evidence)

1. **Office workers and young professionals ordering lunch.**
   - Facebook: "faites vous livrer au bureau ou à la maison".
   - Deliveries start at 12h.
   - Gozem "Vente Flash" ran 12h–13h.

   - Festival stands, Food Challenges.
